This document provides an overview of the data models used to support Thai withholding tax, including the relationships between the required master and transaction data.

## Context

* When business make service payment, Revenue Department required business to collect withholding tax and sent to department at the end of every month
* The deduction rate depends on types of service (rent, advertising, transportation, ...) and party type (individual, company, ...)
