---
title: "Why Standard ERPNext WHT Is Insufficient for Thailand"
importance: Medium
description: Understand why ERPNext's standard withholding tax functionality does not fully meet Thai tax requirements.
---

Standard ERPNext withholding works mainly in two situations:

1. Withholding is recognized on the Purchase Invoice.
2. Withholding is applied to an advance or unallocated Payment Entry.

Thai withholding tax is generally recognized **when payment is made**, including payments allocated to existing Purchase Invoices.

It also lacks key Thai compliance features:

* PND3 and PND53 classification
* Thai income-type and party-specific rates
